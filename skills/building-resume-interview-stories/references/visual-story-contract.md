# Visual Story Contract

Use this contract only for a durable preparation dossier or a project explanation with multiple dependent stages, actors, components, decisions, or validation paths. A single resume bullet, short rewrite, or simple answer does not need a diagram.

## Default visual choices

### Project evidence flow

For a complex project, show:

```text
input / business problem
  → candidate's Confirmed action and decision
  → concrete output
  → validation or quality control
  → supported result or Open result
```

Only the candidate-action nodes may use first-person ownership, and only when they are `Confirmed`. Team or system context is visually separate. `Externally verified`, `Inferred`, `Knowledge supplement`, and `Open` content must remain labelled rather than inheriting the candidate's ownership.

### JD evidence matrix

Use a compact matrix for `JD requirement → Confirmed evidence → transfer logic → gap/Open → interview proof`. A visible gap is a correct output; do not transform a keyword match, public company fact, planned project, or knowledge supplement into experience.

### Technical or collaboration view

Use architecture, role handoff, or decision flow only when the candidate can explain the relevant inputs, outputs, interfaces, controls, and personal boundary. Do not draw internal architecture from public marketing material.

## Placement and acceptance

Place a project flow next to the plain-language project explanation and before its interview questions. Place the JD matrix beside the screening rubric. Keep captions, sources, evidence status, and Open items visible.

For a rendered dossier, use `visual-document-delivery`: read back media and order, inspect every page at normal zoom, and reject clipping, tiny labels, missing captions, empty media, or figures separated from their explanation.

## Acceptance examples

### Complex dossier — visual required

**Input evidence:** A confirmed workflow receives campaign briefs and raw performance data; the candidate confirms definitions, compares two plans, produces a review pack, and records a validation result. Final business adoption remains Open.

**Expected decision:** Create an `input → candidate action → output → validation → result/Open` flow plus a JD evidence matrix. The flow labels the candidate's data checks and comparison as `Confirmed`, team context separately, and adoption as `Open`.

**Failure:** A long project paragraph with no relationship view, or a diagram that labels adoption as the candidate's result.

### One bullet — visual exempt

**Request:** Rewrite one confirmed resume bullet for a supplied JD.

**Expected decision:** Return the concise bullet and necessary truth note. Record `no_visual_reason: single resume bullet with no multi-part durable document relationship`; do not load a rendering workflow or add a decorative image.
